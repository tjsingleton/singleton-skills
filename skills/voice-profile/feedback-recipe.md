# Interview-evidence feedback packet

Use this form for `feedback.md` and discussed-eval writing. Treat the supplied
evaluator record, transcript, notes, and artifacts as the only evidence.

## Packet

### Scope

State the evaluator type, assigned work, attempted work, and delivered work.
Keep assigned, attempted, and delivered separate. Name the candidate **TC** and
use they/them unless the source requires different language.

### What worked

Lead with specific strengths. Tie each claim to a file, hunk, timestamp,
artifact, or observed action. Describe what happened without upgrading
narration, intent, or coached work into demonstrated competence.

### Gaps

List failed assigned partitions with their source counts. Keep undelivered work
separate from attempted failures. If the source contains
`failed_assigned_partitions`, preserve its counts and labels exactly.

### Discussion evidence

Record findings that emerged through interviewer discussion or coaching. Put
discussion verdicts here or in the rationale. Do not add them to scored
partition failures unless the evaluator record scores them there.

### Why

Explain the verdict from the evidence above. Say when the interviewer coached a
defect and soften the claim accordingly. Preserve uncertainty and conflicts in
the source instead of resolving them for a cleaner story.

## Final checks

Before delivery, verify that:

1. Every count, verdict, file, timestamp, and attribution matches the source.
2. Assigned, attempted, delivered, and discussed work remain distinct.
3. Strengths appear before gaps.
4. Coached evidence is labeled and weighted accordingly.
5. The packet does not invent a hire score, confidence score, or personality
   judgment.
