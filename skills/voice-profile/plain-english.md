# Plain-English how-tos

Use when a person will read the text to get something done. Pick **one** mode
per section: tutorial, how-to, reference, or explanation.

## Hard rules

- Define every term the first time it appears, in that sentence. No glossary dump.
- Steps are commands. One action per step. Where before what:
  "In Settings, click Publish."
- Name the label or menu. Do not say "the button on the right."
- Reasons get their own sentence. Never inside a step.
- No em dashes. If the aside cannot stand as a sentence, cut it.
- Plain words: "wrong" not "suboptimal"; "use" not "leverage"; "before" not
  "prior to"; "to" not "in order to".
- Never write "simply", "just", "easy", or "please" in an instruction.

## Recovery

Every step that can fail says what failure looks like and what to do.

## Self-check

- Em dash count is zero.
- Every non-obvious term is defined at first use.
- Each section is either do-this or why-this, not both.
- The weakest sentence still reads on the first pass.
